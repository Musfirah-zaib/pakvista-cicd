Hunza Valley

A breathtaking mountain paradise in northern Pakistan, framed by snow-capped peaks and turquoise lakes.

Rakaposhi Viewpoint

Rakaposhi, meaning "Snow Covered," rises to 7,788 meters and is one of the most stunning peaks visible from the Karakoram Highway. The dedicated viewpoint offers an unobstructed view of the entire mountain from base to summit, making it a favorite stop for travelers and photographers.

Attabad Lake

Formed in 2010 after a massive landslide blocked the Hunza River, Attabad Lake is now famous for its vivid turquoise-blue waters surrounded by dramatic mountains. Visitors can enjoy boating and jet-skiing while taking in one of the most photogenic landscapes in the region.

Baltit Fort

Overlooking Karimabad, Baltit Fort is over 700 years old and was once the residence of the Mirs (rulers) of Hunza. Restored with support from the Aga Khan Trust for Culture, the fort offers panoramic views of the valley and a glimpse into the region's royal history.
