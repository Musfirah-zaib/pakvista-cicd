Karachi – City of Lights

Pakistan's largest metropolis, a vibrant coastal hub blending history, culture, and the Arabian Sea.

Clifton Beach

Clifton Beach is Karachi's most popular seaside destination, stretching along the Arabian Sea. Families gather here for camel rides, food stalls, and stunning sunset views, making it one of the liveliest public spaces in the city.

Mohatta Palace

Built in 1927 as a summer residence, Mohatta Palace is a striking example of Rajasthani-Mughal architecture constructed from pink Jodhpur stone. Today it functions as a museum showcasing art, history, and the cultural heritage of Sindh.

Pakistan Maritime Museum

The Pakistan Maritime Museum is showcases the rich naval heritage of the country, featuring decommissioned ships, submarines, and aircraft. It offers visitors an educational and immersive experience into Pakistan's maritime history and the role of its navy.
