Swat – Switzerland of Pakistan

Lush green valleys, snow-capped peaks, and crystal-clear rivers earn Swat its famous nickname.

Malam Jabba

Malam Jabba is home to Pakistan's premier ski resort, located at an altitude of over 2,800 meters. In winter, the slopes attract skiers and snowboarders from across the country, while in summer the area transforms into a lush hiking and sightseeing destination.

Mingora Bazaar

As the largest city in Swat, Mingora's bustling bazaar is the commercial heart of the valley. Visitors can find traditional handicrafts, embroidered textiles, and local gemstones, offering an authentic taste of Swati culture and trade.

Swat Museum

The Swat Museum in Saidu Sharif houses an impressive collection of Gandhara-era Buddhist artifacts, sculptures, and relics dating back over two thousand years. It serves as a key center for preserving the region's ancient Buddhist heritage.
