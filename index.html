About Pakistan's Tourism

Pakistan is home to some of the most diverse landscapes on Earth — towering Himalayan and Karakoram peaks, lush green valleys, centuries-old Mughal architecture, and vibrant metropolitan cities along the Arabian Sea. From the cultural heart of Lahore to the serene beauty of Hunza Valley, every region tells a unique story of history, nature, and hospitality.

PakVista is your curated guide to five of the country's most loved destinations. Explore each page to learn about iconic landmarks, local culture, and why these places belong on every traveler's bucket list.
