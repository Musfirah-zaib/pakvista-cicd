Lahore – City of Gardens

The cultural heart of Pakistan, rich in Mughal history, vibrant bazaars, and timeless architecture.

Badshahi Mosque

Built in 1673 by the Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb, the Badshahi Mosque is one of the largest mosques in the world and a masterpiece of Mughal architecture. Its red sandstone facade and white marble domes attract thousands of visitors and worshippers every year, especially during Ramadan.

Lahore Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila) showcases over a thousand years of history through its palaces, gardens, and the famous Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors). It stands as a symbol of the grandeur of the Mughal Empire in the Punjab region.

Shalimar Gardens

Laid out in 1641, the Shalimar Gardens are a series of terraced lawns, fountains, and pavilions that reflect classical Mughal garden design. The gardens remain a peaceful retreat in the busy city and are also recognized by UNESCO for their historic and artistic value.
